Captain Willard spends most of *Apocalypse Now* on a patrol boat heading upriver. If we had to choose one watch for that journey, it would need to survive water, mud and heat, and stay readable in the dark.

## Why Marathon

Marathon has supplied watches to the US and Canadian armed forces for decades. That is exactly the kind of background a character like Willard would trust: a tool, not a statement.

## Why the SSNAV

A diver with tritium illumination, a solid case and nothing superfluous. It is the watch that answers every challenge the character faces, from the river to the final scene.
