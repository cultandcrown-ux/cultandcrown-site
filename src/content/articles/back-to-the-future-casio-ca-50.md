Few watches say "this is the eighties" faster than a Casio calculator watch. In *Back to the Future*, Marty McFly wears one, and it does exactly the job a costume piece should do: it places him in time before he goes back in time.

## Costume, not jewellery

Costume designers think in eras. A digital watch with a keypad was the gadget of the moment, affordable and slightly nerdy, a good fit for a teenager who plays guitar and hangs out with a scientist.

## Why it still works

The CA-50 is a cheap watch that became a collectible because of one film. It is also one of the easiest screen-worn references to own today, which is part of why it keeps performing so well with our audience.
