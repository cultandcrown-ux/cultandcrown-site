The rule of this series is simple: three watches, all seen in a film or a TV series, within a fixed budget. Diver, everyday, and something elegant or unusual. This first episode starts small.

## The diver: Seiko SKX009

Seen on Robert Redford in *All Is Lost*. Discontinued, so today you will find it on the second-hand market only.

## The everyday: Casio CA-50

Marty McFly's calculator watch from *Back to the Future*. Cheap, fun and instantly recognisable.

## The different one: Hamilton Boulton Quartz

From *Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny*. A tonneau case that feels nothing like the other two, which is the point.
